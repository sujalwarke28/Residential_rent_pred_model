  NOTE: Linear Regression attains the lowest test RMSE (₹27,897 vs ₹32,716) and the highest test R² (0.792 vs 0.714), while Gradient Boosting attains the lowest test MAE (₹10,135 vs ₹10,760) and MAPE (26.7% vs 28.4%). The mechanism is extrapolation: a linear model fitted in log space keeps extending its trend beyond the range of rents it was trained on, so it gets closer on the handful of very expensive test listings that dominate squared error. A tree ensemble cannot extrapolate at all -- its predictions are bounded by the leaf values it learned -- so it under-predicts those few properties and pays heavily in RMSE, while fitting the other ~99% of listings more accurately. Since the deployed tool estimates rent for ordinary listings, the model with the lower typical error is selected, and the RMSE trade-off is documented rather than hidden.
